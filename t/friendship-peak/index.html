Friendship Peak Trek is a challenging Himalayan summit expedition in Himachal Pradesh, taking you deep into the spectacular Pir Panjal range. Starting from the beautiful Solang Valley near Manali, the trail passes through lush forests, alpine meadows, rocky terrain, and high-altitude landscapes before reaching the snow-covered summit. The trek combines scenic trekking with the thrill of a technical summit climb. As you gain altitude, the landscape transforms dramatically, offering breathtaking views of surrounding Himalayan peaks and valleys. The final summit push demands strong endurance, physical fitness, acclimatization, and the ability to handle challenging terrain and changing mountain conditions.
